## About DataHub

The DataHub platform is a web-based resource for sharing heterogeneous scientific research datasets, models or simulations, processes and research outcomes. More details about SEEK can be found on the [DataHub Website](https://seek4science.org).

## Getting DataHub

To find out how to install DataHub on your own machine, or install DataHub as a Virtual Machine, please go to [Getting DataHub](get-seek.html)
